About Berlin

Precision engineering. Real results.

Based in Pune, Berlin Machineries Private Limited has been a recognised engineering house since 2005. The company supports Indian manufacturers with CNC and EDM machinery built for continuous production, repeatability and long service life.

From conventional machine installations to specialised EDM platforms, Berlin Machineries has built its reputation on reliable products, responsive service and a deep understanding of Indian tool rooms.
